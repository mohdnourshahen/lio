# Lōn Visuals site — working rules

## Content is locked: add only, never remove
Everything currently on the site is approved by the studio and fixed. Projects,
images, captions, covers, page colours, order and copy stay as they are.

- New material from the studio is **added** to what exists. Never delete,
  replace or reorder existing images, blocks, projects or copy as a side effect
  of an addition.
- Change or remove something only when the studio explicitly asks for that
  specific item, in so many words.
- When adding images to a case study, place them where they serve the story,
  and never repeat an image within a page (the hero included).

## Where content lives
- Legacy case studies: generated by `scripts/build_content.py` from the legacy
  pages, with studio curation in `GALLERY`, `ALTS`, `HEROES` and `curate()`.
- Hand-authored case studies: `content/manual/*.json`. These are already in
  final form and are merged as they are.
- Homepage cards, covers and hover images: `selectedWork` in `app/utils/site.ts`.
- Images: `public/media/` and `media.json`, encoded by `scripts/optimise_media.py`.

## Shipping
Preview on the branch first. Merge to `main` (which deploys to Fly at
https://lio.fly.dev) only when the studio says to publish.
